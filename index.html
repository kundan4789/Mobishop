Shipping & returns

Shipping policy +

We ship all over India and work to get the best rates for our customers. Orders placed Monday to Saturday (IST) are processed the same or following business day and shipped within two business days of order placement, if the product is in stock.

Delivery timeline is 4–9 days maximum. Shipping charges vary by order value and delivery location. Questions before ordering? Message us on WhatsApp or email go.mobishopper@gmail.com.

Return & replacement +

Request a replacement or return within 7 days of delivery if:

  • The product is defective, cracked or physically damaged
  • The model, colour or item received doesn't match your order
  • The item is in original condition with original packaging and proof of purchase

How to request: record a short unboxing video, then share your Order ID and the video/photo on WhatsApp at +91 9718 987 099 or email go.mobishopper@gmail.com. We verify and dispatch a free replacement. If a replacement is out of stock, a full refund is processed within 1–3 business days to your original payment mode.

Not returnable: items damaged by misuse, drops or improper installation; products returned without original packaging; clearance-sale items. Orders can be cancelled only before dispatch.
